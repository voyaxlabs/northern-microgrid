## Supported result

This is a reproducible, simulation-only four-site synthetic camp case study. It implements no new optimization algorithm and establishes no measured-site validation, client delivery, professional engineering approval or deployment. Costs, analytic loads and coefficients are stipulated assumptions. Optimization means lowest NPC on the enumerated capacity grid under the fixed dispatcher and diesel sizing.

Corrected renewable delivery in 2018 is 43.9% (Iqaluit), 44.0% (Inuvik), 50.3% (Whitehorse) and 53.4% (Dease Lake). NPC savings remain 11.0%, 12.6%, 17.7% and 18.4%, respectively. H1's cost threshold is met at Whitehorse and Dease Lake under default economics and the 2018 comparison; all evaluated years meet the reliability threshold. H2 is not met. H3 and the matched research-station comparison are untested. The intervals describe the fixed-design five-weather-year mean, with no calibrated coverage or lifecycle uncertainty claim.

Sensitivity changes the conclusion: fuel price -25% yields only 2.01-8.81% savings; CAPEX +25% yields 4.27-11.24%; battery year-12/diesel year-15 replacement yields 6.83-14.10%. H1's 15% cost threshold fails at every site under each of these stresses. Capacities are held fixed and baselines stressed consistently. No robust procurement-savings claim follows.
